서윤 업로더(Seoyun Uploader)는 Honey Studio 운영자가 자신이 소유한 YouTube 채널에 직접 만든 영상을 올리고 제목·설명·썸네일·재생목록·공개 설정을 관리하기 위해 만든 내부용 도구입니다.
Seoyun Uploader is an internal tool built and used by the operator of Honey Studio to upload videos the operator produced to YouTube channels the operator owns, and to manage their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and publishing settings.
